# Outreach Templates

Short, honest, no pitch. Personalize the [bracket] in every send.

## EN — direct (X/Telegram/Discord DM)

> Hi [name] — I'm researching how merchants who accept Stellar/USDC handle *repeat* customers (second month, renewals, top-ups). Saw [their product / a post of theirs]. Could I ask you about how that works for you today? 25–30 min call or async questions, whichever you prefer. We're building open-source tooling in this space and publishing what we learn — happy to share findings either way.

## EN — community post (Discord #payments / forum)

> **Research: how do Stellar merchants handle repeat payments?**
> We're interviewing 10 merchants/operators who accept USDC on Stellar about what happens after the *first* payment — renewals, second charges, stipends, collections. 30 min, findings published openly (anonymized). If you bill anyone more than once, we want to hear how — especially if your current process is a spreadsheet and WhatsApp. DM me or reply here.
