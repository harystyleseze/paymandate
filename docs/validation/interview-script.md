# Interview Script (30 min)

## Rules
- **Never pitch first.** The product is not mentioned until section 4. If they ask, say "we're researching how repeat payments work for Stellar merchants" and move on.
- **Open-ended only.** No yes/no questions until the commitment ask.
- **Past behavior over hypotheticals.** "What did you do last month" beats "would you use".
- Record (with consent) or take verbatim notes; fill `tracking.md` within 24h.

## 1. Context (5 min)
- What does your business sell, and how do customers pay you today?
- Roughly how many customers paid you more than once in the last 3 months?

## 2. The core five (15 min)
1. "Walk me through exactly what happens, step by step, when a customer needs to pay you for the **second** month."
2. "How many hours did your team spend last month getting repeat customers to pay, and who did that work?"
3. "What have you already tried or built to automate repeat payments — and why did you stop using it?"
4. "What percentage of customers who paid once never paid again, and what do you believe is the reason?"
5. "If repeat charges happened automatically, what would you worry about going wrong?"

## 3. Probes (as needed)
- "You said it takes [X hours] — what's the most painful single step?"
- "When a customer's payment fails or is late, what literally happens next?"
- "Show me the spreadsheet / message template if you're comfortable." (Artifacts = strongest evidence)

## 4. Only now: the concept + commitment ask (5 min)
- One sentence: "Customer signs once; you can charge them up to a capped amount each month; they can revoke anytime; settles in USDC."
- "What's your first reaction — and what would stop you from using it?"
- **Commitment ask:** "We'll have this on testnet in a few weeks. Would you pilot it with a handful of real customers?" (Only an unprompted-enthusiasm yes counts for the gate; polite yeses score 0.)

## 5. Always close with
- "Who else do you know who deals with this?" (referral = secondary demand signal)
- "Can I follow up when we publish what we learned?"

## Scoring (per interview, into tracking.md)
- Manual recurring workflow present? (Y/N + hrs/month)
- Workaround built/bought? (Y/N + what)
- Failed second payments a stated churn cause? (Y/N)
- Pilot commitment? (none / polite / unprompted-enthusiastic)
- Verbatim quotes worth keeping
